# GLBA Explained: Does it Apply to You? Coverage, Privacy, and Safeguards

## The Threshold Question: "Significantly Engaged"

A business is a "financial institution" under GLBA if it is **significantly engaged** in financial activities under BHC Act §4(k). Two factors:

- **Formal arrangement** — Is the financial activity offered through a formal, ongoing arrangement or product? (e.g., issuing a credit card, wire transfer service) vs. informal tab.
- **Frequency** — How regularly does the business engage in the activity? (e.g., regular money transmission vs. occasional layaway).

Stronger/more frequent activity = likely covered. Neither factor is a bright line; FTC weighs both.

## Second Form of Coverage

Even if not a financial institution, you are subject to **reuse/redisclosure limits** if you receive NPI from a nonaffiliated financial institution.
