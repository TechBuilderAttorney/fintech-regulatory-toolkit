### GLBA Safeguards Rule – Explanatory Note

**Source:** FTC Safeguards Rule, 16 C.F.R. Part 314 (updated May 2024).

**Rule:** Covered financial institutions must develop, implement, and maintain a comprehensive **written information security program (WISP)**. Applies to any business “significantly engaged” in financial activities under GLBA—same coverage test as the Privacy Rule.

**Core requirements:**
- **WISP** – Written program appropriate to size and complexity.
- **Administrative, technical, and physical safeguards** – Designated employee(s) to oversee; risk assessment; employee training; service provider oversight.
- **Multi-factor authentication (MFA)** – Required for any information system accessed by employees.
- **Annual board report** – At least annually, report on overall security program status and compliance.
- **Service provider oversight** – Contractual requirements; periodic assessments.
- **Breach notification** – Notify FTC within **30 days** of discovering a security event affecting **500+ consumers**.

**Key distinction:** Safeguards Rule = security of NPI. Privacy Rule = notice, opt-out, and sharing limits. Both apply to financial institutions.
