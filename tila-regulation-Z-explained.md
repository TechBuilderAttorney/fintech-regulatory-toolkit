# TILA / Regulation Z

**Source:** CFPB, 12 CFR Part 1026 (Regulation Z), implementing the Truth in Lending Act.

**Rule:** Requires clear, upfront disclosure of key credit terms before a consumer becomes obligated on consumer credit.

**Core disclosures:** APR, finance charge, amount financed, total of payments, payment schedule.

**Timing:** Before consummation (closed-end); at account opening and via periodic statements (open-end).

**Coverage:** Consumer credit – mortgages, HELOCs, credit cards, installment loans, certain student loans.

**Exemption threshold:** Higher-priced mortgage loan threshold for 2026 is **$73,400** (adjusts annually).

**EWA (Earned Wage Access):** May not be "credit" under TILA — evolving area.

**Reg Z subparts:**
- Subpart B – open-end credit
- Subpart C – closed-end credit
- Subpart E – mortgages
- Subpart F – reverse mortgages

**Right of rescission:** For certain home-secured loans, consumer can cancel within 3 business days.

**Advertising:** Triggering terms (e.g., "no down payment," specific APR) require additional disclosures.

**Common pitfalls:** Late fees, origination fees, misleading advertising, failure to disclose before commitment.

**Relevance to Airwallex:** Applies if Airwallex extends consumer credit (embedded lending, BNPL, credit lines). Cross-border products need localized disclosures.

**Save:** TILA/Reg Z = APR + finance charge + amount financed + total of payments, disclosed before consumer commits. Consumer credit only.
